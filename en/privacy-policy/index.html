Privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026.

Your privacy matters to me. This page explains what data I collect when you contact me or use this website, why I collect it and how I handle it.

Who is responsible for your data

Soul Space, massage studio, Diana Lendić, Sinjska 5, 21000 Split, Croatia. For any questions about privacy you can reach me at soul.space.split@gmail.com.

What data I collect

The website itself does not require any registration and does not collect your data automatically. I only receive data when you contact me yourself, by message on WhatsApp, Instagram, email or by phone. I can then see:

  • your name and contact details (phone number, email or Instagram profile),
  • the content of the message you send me (e.g. how you feel and what is troubling you, so that I can adapt the ritual).

Why and on what basis

I use the data solely to reply to you, arrange a session and prepare the ritual for you. The basis for this is your enquiry (taking steps at your request) and my legitimate interest in communicating with clients. I do not use the data for marketing and I do not share it with any third party.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools. The exception is the map on the contact page (Google Maps), which may set Google cookies when it loads. I have no control over them. You can read more in Google's privacy policy.

Third-party apps

If you contact me via WhatsApp or Instagram, the communication takes place through those apps and is subject to their privacy rules (WhatsApp, Instagram).

How long I keep the data

I keep messages and contact details for as long as we are in touch, that is, for as long as there is a need to arrange sessions. You can ask me to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

  • request access to the data I hold about you,
  • request correction or deletion of the data,
  • object to the processing,
  • lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (azop.hr).

For any of the above, just write to me at soul.space.split@gmail.com.
