about

Diana Lendić

Certified massage therapist

All my life I have been in love with movement. Dance, contortion and exploring what my own body could do were a big part of my life. I thought I knew my body because through it I could dance, create and explore movement.

Only later did I realise there is a completely different relationship with the body. One in which the body is not just a tool for movement but the place we live in. The place that holds my emotions, memories, boundaries, sadness, joy and sense of safety.

After several years of my own therapy and three years of training in body-oriented psychotherapy, I wanted to deepen my understanding of the body through touch. I enrolled in massage school and later continued training in various courses and manual techniques.

Today I work exactly the way I wish someone had worked with me many years ago. With full presence, respect for boundaries and trust that the body knows its own rhythm when we give it enough time, attention and a safe space.

I don't know which ritual to choose. what should I do?

Do not worry if you are not sure what to choose. In our short opening conversation we will work out together what your body needs and pick the ritual that makes the most sense that day.

how should I prepare for my visit?

Hardly at all. The only small preparation is a warm shower before you come. Your skin then takes in the oil and the touch best, and your body relaxes faster.

is the massage painful?

No. The pressure is firm when your body asks for it but never rough. I work at a pace your nervous system can follow.
