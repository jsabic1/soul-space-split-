my rituals

I call them rituals for a reason. Women used to have their own: baths, oils and evenings when no one was in a hurry. Today even rest is squeezed in between two obligations, phone in hand.

That is why each one here begins with a conversation, lasts as long as it needs to and ends with a cup of tea, so you leave calmly and not in a rush.

How I work

hands first, then everything else

At the start I simply rest my palms on you and wait. In a day, hardly anyone touches us without needing something, so those few moments tell me more about how you are than any questionnaire.

no sudden movements

I move in a steady rhythm so you always know what comes next. Messages, alarms and deadlines startle us enough during the day, so predictability is often all it takes to let go.

your “no” is enough

Tell me if the pressure is too strong, if you are cold or if something simply does not feel right, and I will change it straight away. Many of us were taught to say everything is fine even when it is not, which is why I also ask you myself during the massage.
