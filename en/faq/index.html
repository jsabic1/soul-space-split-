FAQ

01

how do I book a session?

The easiest way is to tap the “Book a session” button anywhere on the site and WhatsApp will open with a message already written. You can also message me on Instagram or by email. I reply within 24 hours and we agree on a time.

02

I don't know which ritual to choose. what should I do?

Do not worry if you are not sure what to choose. In our short opening conversation we will work out together what your body needs and pick the ritual that makes the most sense that day.

03

how should I prepare for my visit?

Hardly at all. The only small preparation is a warm shower before you come. Your skin then takes in the oil and the touch best, and your body relaxes faster. Everything else we arrange on the spot, at your pace.

04

is the massage painful?

No. The pressure is firm when your body asks for it but never rough. I work at a pace your nervous system can follow. If your body is not ready to open up an area, we do not go there. You have the final say, always.

05

what if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Message me as soon as you can, at the latest 12 hours before your session, and we will simply arrange a new one. Cancelling within 12 hours is charged at 50% of the ritual price, and a no-show without notice at the full price. More in the cancellation policy.

06

where is the studio?

At Sinjska 5, 21000 Split. Tap the address to open Google Maps.

07

how long does a ritual last?

Between 30 and 120 minutes, depending on the ritual. The exact duration is listed with each ritual on the Rituals page. Leave yourself a little space afterwards. It is best not to rush straight on.
